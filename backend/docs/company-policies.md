# Novatech Solutions — Company Policies

Internal policies for Novatech Solutions employees. (Demo document — replace
the files in this folder with your real company documents and re-run
`npm run ingest`.)

## Vacation policy

Employees receive 15 paid vacation days per year. Vacation requests must be
submitted at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal and approved by
the direct manager.

## Refund policy

Customers may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days,
refunds are issued as account credit only. Refunds over 5,000 USD require
approval from the finance department, and are recorded as negative amounts in
the transactions ledger.

## Working hours

Standard working hours are 09:00–18:00, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour
lunch break. Remote work is allowed up to 2 days per week with manager
approval.
