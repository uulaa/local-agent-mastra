# Novatech Solutions — Company Overview

Novatech Solutions is a B2B software company selling cloud software modules,
hardware, and support services to around 300 business customers worldwide,
from startups to enterprise accounts in North America, Europe, and Asia
Pacific (including Mongolia).

## What we sell

- **Subscription plans**: Starter, Growth, Business, Pro, and Enterprise
  tiers, billed monthly or annually. Prices vary by region (Global, EU, APAC).
- **Products**: software modules (analytics, dashboards, API gateways,
  monitoring), hardware, one-off services, and support packages.

## How billing works

- Every subscription generates a monthly invoice. Invoices are due 14 days
  after the issue date; unpaid invoices past the due date are marked overdue.
- Invoices include a 10% tax on the subtotal.
- Payments are accepted by card, bank transfer, PayPal, and wire.
- The finance team records refunds, credits, and adjustments as transactions
  in the ledger.

## Teams

- **Sales**: ~50 internal staff (sales reps, account managers, support
  agents) handle direct, online, partner, and reseller channels.
- **Support**: customer tickets are tracked by category (billing, technical,
  account, feature request) and priority (low to urgent). Urgent tickets must
  receive a first response within 2 business hours.
